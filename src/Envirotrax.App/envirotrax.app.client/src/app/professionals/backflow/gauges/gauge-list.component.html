Please read the following important information on how to register and manage your gauges.

Register your gauge

Please enter the manufacturer, model, serial number, and select whether the gauge will be used on potable or non-potable water systems. You must upload an electronic copy of the Test for Accuracy (TFA) report to your account by clicking on the Choose File button and selecting the TFA file to upload, or drag and drop the file into the box. Click the Register New Gauge button below to register a new gauge with your account.

Gauge validation

The gauge will not be available for use until the test for accuracy expiration date is entered by Envirotrax® personnel into the system. Test for accuracy reports are normally validated within an hour but could take as long as one business day on rare occasions.

Please click on the Choose File button below and select the Test for Accuracy Report (TFA) you wish to upload.
