Please read the following important information on how to register and manage your company’s insurance policies.

Adding Insurance Policies

Use the form below to add a new insurance policy to your account.

The following applies to all “Master” and User Accounts:

Insurance policies registered within a "master" account will be applied to all User Accounts. Insurance policy registration is not necessary for each individual User Account. The Company Name on the account must match the company name on the Certificate of Insurance (COI).

Verify that the information is correct and click the Register New Insurance Policy button to begin registering your insurance policy.

You must upload an electronic copy of your Certificate of Insurance (COI) to your account by clicking in the Choose a File area and selecting the COI file to upload, or dragging a file into the area. Once we receive the file, our staff will validate the policy by entering the expiration date and amount of coverage.

Renewing an Insurance Policy

For insurance policy renewals, you must enter the new policy number and upload an electronic copy of your Certificate of Insurance (COI) to your account. Policies are normally validated within an hour, but could take as long as one business day on rare occasions.
