Please read the following important information on how to register and manage your licenses & company insurance policy.

Adding Licenses & Insurance Policies

Use the form below to add a new license or insurance policy to your account.

The following applies to all “Master” and sub accounts:

  1. A BPAT license must be added for each account or sub account in order for the account to be authorized for use in backflow test submissions. The Company Name and/or Contact Name on the account must match the name of the license holder. If a "master" account is only used for sub account administration and not backflow test submissions, a BPAT license does not need to be registered for the account.
  2. Insurance policies registered with a "master" account will be applied to all of its sub accounts. Insurance policy registration is not necessary for each individual sub account. The Company Name on the account must match the company name on the Certificate of Insurance (COI).

Verify that the information is correct and click the Register New License or Insurance Policy button to begin the process of registering your license or insurance policy.

  1. All BPAT and fire company licenses will be verified by Vepo personnel. Once the license is verified, our staff will validate the license by entering in the expiration date on file with the authority issuing the license.
    Note: If your company intends to test fireline assemblies in the State of Texas, you certify that BPATs registered in your account are permanent employees per §290.44(h)(4)(A)(ii).
  2. If you are registering an insurance policy you must upload an electronic copy of your Certificate of Insurance (COI) to your account by clicking on the Choose File button and selecting the COI file to upload. Once we receive the file, our staff will validate the policy by entering the expiration date.

Renewing Your Licenses & Insurance Policies

  1. All BPAT and fire company licenses are monitored by Vepo personnel and updated near the time of their expiration. If a license is not renewed, we will not be able to update the license and your account will become unauthorized for use.
  2. For insurance policy renewals, you must enter the new policy number and upload an electronic copy of your Certificate of Insurance (COI) to your account by clicking on the Choose File button and selecting the COI file to upload. Policies are normally validated within an hour, but could take as long as one business day on rare occasions.

Reinstating Expired Licenses & Insurance Policies

  1. Vepo personnel will continue to check for renewals of expired BPAT and fire company licenses for a set period of time. If the license is not renewed within that set time period, the license will no longer be checked for renewal and you must reinstate the license manually. To reinstate an expired license you must first renew your license with the authority issuing the license. Then, contact us so that we can verify the license and update it within the system.

Adding Insurance Policies

When registering an insurance policy, you must upload an electronic copy of your Certificate of Insurance (COI) by clicking the Choose File button and selecting the file to upload. Once received, our staff will validate the policy by entering the expiration date.

Verify that all information is correct, then click the Register New Insurance Policy button to submit your policy for review.

Renewing Your Insurance Policies

To renew an insurance policy, enter the new policy number and upload an updated Certificate of Insurance (COI). Policies are typically validated within an hour, but may take up to one business day in rare cases.

Reinstating Expired Insurance Policies

If your insurance policy has expired, you must obtain a renewed policy from your insurance provider before reinstating it in the system. Once you have the updated policy, contact us so our staff can verify and update it.

Please click on the Choose File button below and select the Certificate of Insurance (COI) you wish to upload.
