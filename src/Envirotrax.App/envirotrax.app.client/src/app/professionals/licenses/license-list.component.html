Please read the following important information on how to register and manage your licenses.

Adding Licenses

Use the form below to add a new license to your account.

The following applies to all “Master” and sub accounts:

  1. A BPAT license must be added for each account or sub account in order for the account to be authorized for use in backflow test submissions. The Company Name and/or Contact Name on the account must match the name of the license holder. If a "master" account is only used for sub account administration and not backflow test submissions, a BPAT license does not need to be registered for the account.
  2. Fire company licenses are registered for the company and are not tied to a specific account.

Verify that the information is correct and click the Add New License button to begin the process of registering your license.

  1. All BPAT and fire company licenses will be verified by Vepo personnel. Once the license is verified, our staff will validate the license by entering in the expiration date on file with the authority issuing the license.
    Note: If your company intends to test fireline assemblies in the State of Texas, you certify that BPATs registered in your account are permanent employees per §290.44(h)(4)(A)(ii).

Renewing Your Licenses

  1. All BPAT and fire company licenses are monitored by Vepo personnel and updated near the time of their expiration. If a license is not renewed, we will not be able to update the license and your account will become unauthorized for use.

Reinstating Expired Licenses

  1. Vepo personnel will continue to check for renewals of expired BPAT and fire company licenses for a set period of time. If the license is not renewed within that set time period, the license will no longer be checked for renewal and you must reinstate the license manually. To reinstate an expired license you must first renew your license with the authority issuing the license. Then, contact us so that we can verify the license and update it within the system.

Adding a Registration

Use the form below to add a new registration to your account.

The following applies to all “Master” and sub accounts:

  1. A Sludge Transporter Registration number must be added for each master account in order for the account to be authorized to submit trip tickets. The Sludge Transporter Registration number of a "master" account will be applied to all of its sub accounts. The Company Name on the account must match the company name on the Sludge Transporter Registration. Our staff will validate the number by entering the expiration date.

Renewing Your Registration

  1. For a Sludge Transporter Registration renewal, you must enter the registration number. Registrations are normally validated within an hour but could take up to one business day on rare occasions.
