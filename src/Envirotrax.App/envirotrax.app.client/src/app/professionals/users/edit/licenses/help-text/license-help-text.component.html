Please read the following important information on how to register and manage your licenses.

Adding Licenses

Use the form below to add a new license for this professional. All license types, including Backflow, Fire, CSI, Plumbing, and others, are managed from this single location.

Each license must be registered for the professional to whom it belongs. The name on the professional's account must match the name on the license.

Once submitted, all licenses will be verified by Envirotrax® personnel. Our staff will validate the license with the issuing authority and enter the expiration date.

Note: If your company intends to test fire line assemblies in the State of Texas, you certify that BPATs registered in your account are permanent employees per §290.44(h)(4)(A)(ii).

Renewing Your Licenses

All licenses will be monitored by Envirotrax® personnel and updated near the time of their expiration. If a license is not renewed, we will not be able to update the expiration date, and your account will become unauthorized for use.

Reinstating Expired Licenses

Envirotrax® personnel will continue to check for renewals of expired licenses for a set period of time. If the license is not renewed within that period, it will be removed from the account. To reinstate an expired license, first renew it with the issuing authority, then resubmit the license through the online system.
