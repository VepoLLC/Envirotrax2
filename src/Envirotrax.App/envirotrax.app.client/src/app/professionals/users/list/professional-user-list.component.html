User Account Management

If your company employs more than one professional, you will need to create a separate User Account for each individual.

Your company account will serve as the Master Account (Owner/Manager), allowing you to manage the User Accounts associated with your company.

Each professional will receive an invitation to register and create their own login credentials, including a User ID (email address) and password.

Benefits of Creating User Accounts

  • Each User Account is automatically registered with all water suppliers associated with the Master Account, eliminating the need for individual registrations.
  • Each professional has their own User ID and password, allowing them to access Envirotrax® and submit reports under their individual account.
  • The Master Account holder or an administrator can manage associated User Accounts and remove individuals who are no longer employed by the company.

Removing a User Account

If a professional leaves your company or is terminated, the Master Account holder or an administrator can remove their User Account at any time. When a User Account is removed:

  • All reports previously submitted by that individual are automatically transferred to the Master Account, preserving the company's reporting history.
  • The individual will no longer have access to those reports.
  • The individual's User Account will be removed from the system and will no longer be associated with the Master Account, its water supplier registrations, licenses, insurance, test gauges, or vehicle information.
